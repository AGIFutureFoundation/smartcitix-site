8 · Meet the nine advisors — your coach cast

The Holodeck Coach above is a guide on this page. Inside the halls, a different cast waits: nine scripted advisors who stand in the rooms and on the campus green, each one of the Academy's own rigged avatars, and each able to answer exactly 35 questions between them. An advisor is a scripted guide, not an instructor and not an AI: no model runs behind one, and nothing is generated at view time. An answer is either say (written and cited) or read (bound to a live record, such as the PPE conditions, a seat rubric, or a hall's rooms). Twenty-one of the 35 topics carry no words. Advisors are not scored, and no grader reads advisor state.

10 · How the halls are built — building kit & interiors

The halls are assembled from an 18-piece low-poly building vocabulary — 43 primitives (36 boxes + 7 cylinders), 12–72 triangles per piece (median 34; the whole vocabulary 556 triangles). A hall envelope is 12.0 m wide, 6 + (depth % 3) × 0.7 m high, with its door toward local −z. Measured reference: one residential city block, 21,893 triangles / 93 meshes.

The 18 pieces: Entrance stoop · Entrance tread · Kerb run · Parapet coping run · Parapet corner block · Eaves gutter · Downpipe run · Downpipe shoe · Wall vent hood · Louvred vent panel · Door awning · Bollard · Wall-mounted hand rail · Sign bracket · Surface conduit run · Meter box · Planter tub · Caged roof ladder.

Interiors: six authored custom spaces — a welding bay, a rigging yard, an electrical lab, a scaffold court, a flipped classroom, a crane simulator bay — with floor plans drawn at 40 px/m. Interiors hold 29 props over 7 families, 5,082 instances across 1,221 rooms; wall runs first, then authored islands with a 1.5 m aisle; the 11 room strands mirror the 11 skill strands. Surfaces: 22 floor finishes, and every text-on-plate pair must clear 4.5:1.

Source: SmartCiti.X wiki/Building-Kit.md, wiki/Interiors-Map.md, wiki/Custom-Spaces.md, wiki/Surface-Finishes.md.
